Privacy Policy

Υποκριτές (Imposters) · Effective 29 October 2026

Υποκριτές (“the App”) is a party game played on a single device. It is published by Alex Mylonas (“we”, “us”), who is the data controller for the data described below.

In short

  • There are no accounts, and we never ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Player names stay on your device and are deleted when the App closes.
  • We may collect anonymous usage statistics to improve the game. We don’t sell them or use them for advertising.
  • The App has no ads, no in-app purchases and no tracking across other apps or websites.

1. Player names

The names you type when setting up a game are used only to show them on screen. They aren’t stored permanently or sent to us or anyone else, and they’re gone when the App closes.

2. Anonymous usage statistics

To understand how the game is played and improve it, the App may send anonymous usage statistics to an analytics provider. This includes:

  • events such as opening the App and starting, finishing or abandoning a game,
  • game settings: number of players and imposters, chosen word categories, and whether the “traitor” and the imposter hint are turned on,
  • durations, such as how long a game lasted,
  • App version, operating system and OS version,
  • a random anonymous installation ID, so devices can be told apart without knowing who owns them.

We never send player names, the secret word, the hint, contact details, your location or advertising identifiers (IDFA/AAID).

Provider: Analytics collection is not active yet. Before we turn it on, we’ll update this page with the provider’s name and where the data is stored.

Legal basis: Our legitimate interest in improving the App (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) or, where the law requires it, your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

Retention: Statistics are kept for up to 24 months, then deleted or combined into aggregate totals.

3. Data collected by the app stores

Apple (App Store) and Google (Google Play) may collect data when you download or use apps, for example crash reports if you’ve allowed them in your device settings. Their own privacy policies cover that data.

4. Children

The App is suitable for all ages. We don’t ask for or knowingly collect personal data from children. The statistics in section 2 are anonymous and don’t reveal who is using the device.

5. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, port and object to the processing of your data. Because we don’t know who you are, we may not be able to find your data, but we’ll do our best if you contact us. Uninstalling the App deletes the anonymous installation ID from your device.

You can also complain to the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (www.dpa.gr) or the data protection authority in your country.

6. Security

Statistics are sent encrypted (HTTPS) and can be accessed only by us and the provider that processes them on our behalf.

7. Changes

If the way we handle data changes, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.
